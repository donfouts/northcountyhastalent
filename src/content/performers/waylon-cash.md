Waylon Cash is a Fallbrook artist named "Artist of the Year" four times by the West Coast CMA. Known for his guitar work and heartfelt vocals, he has played Temecula wineries and sold-out shows including The Merc, and was featured on Norman's Rare Guitars' YouTube channel.
