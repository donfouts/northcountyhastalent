Tell us about your act and send a link to a short video of you performing. Our team reviews every submission and will reach out to selected acts by email.

### What we need

- Your name or act name
- A still photo (a link to Instagram, Google Drive, Dropbox, etc. is fine)
- Your website and social media links
- A short bio (2–4 sentences)
- A link to a **publicly viewable** video of you performing live (YouTube preferred; unlisted is fine)

### Good to know

- Performers must be available on event day for sound check.
- Acts of all ages are welcome to apply; the event itself is 21+ for guests.
- Submission deadline and set length: _to be announced_.
